What do you do when you're bored and have access to some pretty unusual equipment? Well, apparently, you make a video game.

Back in 1958, two people at Brookhaven National Laboratory, **William Higinbotham** and **Robert Dvorak**, created a game using laboratory equipment, particularly an oscilloscope, just to entertain visitors.

## Why I'm telling you this

And now, I'm joining this mission of entertainment by learning how to make games and creating one of my own.

So, join the adventure along with me!
